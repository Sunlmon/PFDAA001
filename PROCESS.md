## Tools and Purposes

At the beginning of this assignment, I provided ChatGPT with the assignment brief and course background because I needed help developing possible directions for the essay. I had not used Visual Studio Code before, and I had not fully understood the process of using it with GitHub to publish the assignment. I therefore also asked ChatGPT for step-by-step instructions covering file creation, commits, and uploading.

Although I gave the assistant detailed requests, I initially found its technical instructions difficult to follow. I later found a screen-recorded VS Code and GitHub tutorial on a video platform and reread the assignment brief myself. After gaining a clearer understanding of the task, I asked ChatGPT to help me organise the main ideas and examples in Dylan Beattie’s The Art of Code.

I first developed the argument and language framework in Chinese. ChatGPT was then used mainly to reorganise paragraphs, translate the essay into English, correct grammar and spelling, and check the in-text citation and APA reference format.

## One Idea I Kept

I kept the assistant’s summary of one central idea from The Art of Code: rules, syntax, constraints, and execution can become materials for creative expression.

This idea connected with my own memory of encountering an animated typography work created with code. It helped me explain why learning programming is not only about producing functional software, but also about understanding a medium that can support visual and artistic expression.

## One Idea I Rejected

In an early example, the assistant incorrectly drew on unrelated conversation context and presented projects involving Unity, ESP32-S3, and sensors as if they were my personal experience. Although these examples sounded specific and plausible, they were not experiences that I could truthfully use in this essay.

I rejected and removed them, and then instructed the assistant to work only with information that I personally provided during the current writing process. This mistake showed me that fluent and detailed AI-generated content is not necessarily accurate or relevant. I still needed to verify where each claim came from and take responsibility for every personal experience included in the essay.

## Final Editing and Verification

After identifying my own argument, I reorganised the essay around my memory of seeing a code-generated animated work. I then connected that experience with Beattie’s discussion of code as a possible artistic medium and finally addressed why designers should still learn programming when AI can generate code.

ChatGPT assisted with language editing, translation, spelling, and citation formatting. However, I reviewed the personal experience, argument, and final editorial decisions myself. I also opened the original video link and checked its title, publishing account, date, and reference information.
